Know the Warning Signs

Signs You Need a Termite Inspection

These signs deserve investigation. An inspection helps distinguish termite activity from other insects, moisture damage and ordinary wear.

Hollow-Sounding Wood

Doors, frames or furniture that sound hollow or show internal channels may need assessment. A hollow sound alone does not confirm termite activity.

Pratishkhedekar / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Display cropped.

Mud Tubes on Foundations

Earth-like tunnels along foundations, walls or floor joints may be termite shelter tubes. Have them assessed to determine whether they indicate current activity.

Jim Conrad / Wikimedia Commons · Public domain · Display cropped.

Discarded Wings

Shed wings around windows, doors or lights are worth inspecting. Identification helps distinguish termite swarmers from flying ants and other insects.

RCraig09 / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Display resized.

Damaged or Warped Frames

Soft, crumbling or damaged door frames need inspection. Sticking doors and warping can also have other causes, including moisture and movement.

Entomology, CSIRO / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY 3.0 · Display cropped.

Pellets or Droppings Near Wood

Small pellets near wooden surfaces deserve identification. Their appearance and location help an inspector assess whether they are termite frass or material from another source.

Reference: Pacific dampwood termite pellets. JJ Greive / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Display cropped.

Visible Tunnels in Wood

Exposed channels or insects inside damaged wood are reasons to arrange an inspection. Assessment helps identify the pest and determine the treatment needed.

Pratishkhedekar / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Display cropped.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I discuss termite treatment for a new building?
Contact IBS during planning or before relevant foundation and flooring areas are covered. We can review the construction stage and explain suitable treatment timing. If construction is further along, available options depend on site access and the building layout.
What preparation is needed for an occupied home?
Preparation depends on the selected product and application method. Before treatment, ask about access, moving belongings, protecting food, precautions for children and pets, and re-entry instructions. Follow the product-specific guidance provided by the application team.
What is the difference between termite treatment and termite proofing?
Termite treatment addresses identified activity, while termite proofing usually refers to preventive measures intended to reduce entry risk. The recommended approach depends on your construction stage, site conditions and inspection findings.
How often should termite treatment be repeated?
There is no single repeat-treatment interval for every property. The product, application method, site changes and inspection findings influence the need for further work. Ask IBS for monitoring guidance for your treatment, and arrange an inspection if new signs appear.
