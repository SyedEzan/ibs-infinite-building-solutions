Why It Matters

Professional Termite Treatment in Karachi

Termites, especially subterranean species common in Karachi, live in soil and enter buildings through foundations, cracks, and wooden fixtures. Over time, they weaken doors, frames, furniture, and even structural timber. Because the damage often goes unnoticed until it is advanced, a dependable termite treatment and proofing system is important for any property.

At IBS, we assess the stage of the building and the level of exposure, then apply the appropriate treatment. For structures under construction, we recommend pre-construction soil treatment. For existing buildings, we use post-construction chemical barriers that create a protective line around the property.

Pre-Construction Soil Treatment

Applied at the foundation stage, treating bottom surfaces, column pit sides, and basement floors to prevent termites entering from below.

Post-Construction Chemical Barrier

Drilling and injection of approved anti-termite solution to form inner and outer barriers down to the soil beneath floors.

Wood & Fixture Treatment

Protection for wooden doors, frames, and built-in fixtures that are particularly vulnerable to termite attack.

Pre-Construction

Pre-Construction Termite Treatment

Pre-construction treatment is the most effective way to protect a new building from termites. Applied during the foundation and flooring stages, it treats the soil and contact surfaces before the structure is built, creating a barrier that prevents termites from entering.

  • Foundation-stage treatment of bottom surfaces and column pit sides
  • Basement floor and sub-grade surface treatment
  • Approved anti-termite chemical application at controlled rates
  • Long-term protection built into the structure

Best For

  • Developers and contractors

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does termite treatment last in Pakistan?

IBS termite proofing comes with a 10+ year written guarantee. Our certified chemical soil barriers and post-construction treatments are designed to prevent re-infestation for over a decade.

What is the difference between pre-construction and post-construction treatment?

Pre-construction treatment is applied during the foundation stage before the building is built, while post-construction treatment is applied to existing buildings through chemical barrier injection around the structure.

Can termite treatment be done in an occupied home?

Yes. We plan treatments around occupants and explain the preparation and safety steps before work begins.

How do I know if my building has termites?

Common signs include mud tubes along walls or foundations, hollow-sounding wood, visible damage to door frames and furniture, and small piles of wings near windows.
