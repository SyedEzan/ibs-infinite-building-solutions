Know the Warning Signs

Signs Your Property Needs a Pest Inspection

These clues help guide an inspection. A single sign does not always identify the pest or confirm an infestation.

Droppings & Smear Marks

Unexplained droppings or marks near cupboards, food storage and wall edges need identification and assessment.

Chewed Packaging or Wiring

Gnaw marks on food packets, boxes or cables may indicate rodent activity. Damaged electrical wiring also needs assessment by a qualified electrician.

Unusual Noises at Night

Repeated scratching or movement around ceilings, walls and storage areas is worth investigating to identify its source.

Marks on Bedding

Dark spotting, shed skins or insects around mattress seams and bed frames need inspection. Bites alone cannot confirm bed bugs.

Repeated Pest Sightings

Frequently seeing insects or rodents in the same area helps identify where an inspection and monitoring should focus.

Nesting Material & Egg Cases

Shredded materials, suspected nests or insect egg cases around concealed areas are reasons to arrange a closer assessment.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a pest control treatment take?
Timing depends on the property size, pest type, access and selected method. IBS can provide a time estimate after reviewing the treatment scope. Some problems require further visits and monitoring.
What preparation is needed before treatment?
Preparation depends on the treatment. Ask about storing food, clearing access, moving belongings and precautions for children and pets. Bed bug preparation can differ from kitchen pest treatment, so follow the checklist provided for your visit.
When can I return to my property after treatment?
Re-entry depends on the product, application method and site conditions. Follow the specific instructions given by the treatment team. Do not use a general waiting time as a substitute for the product's re-entry requirements.
Do you offer scheduled pest control for businesses?
Yes. IBS offers scheduled pest management enquiries for restaurants, offices, warehouses and shops. The visit frequency and treatment scope are discussed according to the pest activity and business requirements.
