XPS Insulation Board

Premium extruded polystyrene board for high-performance thermal insulation, moisture resistance, and long-term durability across roofs, walls, and slabs.

Request Technical Quote View All Products
Insulation

Thermal performance with moisture protection

IBS XPS Insulation Board is engineered to deliver superior compressive strength, stable R-values, and resistance to water absorption. It keeps interiors cooler during summer and helps reduce energy use in hot climates.

Key advantages

  • High compressive strength for roof loading and traffic
  • Low water absorption and long-term thermal stability
  • Excellent vapor resistance and insulation continuity
  • Easy to cut, install, and seal on roof assemblies

Product at a glance

Designed for roof, wall, and floor assemblies where thermal comfort and moisture resistance are critical.

  • Thickness range: 20mm to 100mm
  • Thermal conductivity: 0.028 W/m·K
  • Compressive strength: 300 - 500 kPa
  • Water absorption: <0.7%

Where XPS Board works best

Use it in finished roof gardens, inverted roofs, balconies, terraces, and external walls to create a stable thermal envelope with reliable moisture control.

Roof insulation

Protect roof slabs against heat gain while maintaining compressive strength for foot traffic and roof finishes.

Wall systems

Improve external wall insulation and reduce thermal bridge risk with rigid board layers.

Basement slabs

Provide a continuous insulation layer under slabs with low water absorption and long-term stability.

Technical features

Thermal efficiency

Low thermal conductivity reduces heat transfer and improves indoor comfort.

Water resistance

Highly closed-cell structure prevents water absorption and maintains insulation value.

Durability

Resists compressive deformation, ensuring long service life under loads.

Applications and installation

  • Flat and pitched roof assemblies
  • Green roof systems and rooftop terraces
  • External insulated wall cladding
  • Basement floor insulation and internal slabs

Frequently asked questions

Can XPS board be used under a waterproofing membrane?

Yes. XPS is ideal beneath membranes because it stays dry and stabilizes the roof assembly.

How much temperature reduction can it deliver?

Installed correctly, XPS can reduce roof surface heat gain significantly and help lower cooling loads.

Is the board suitable for high-foot-traffic roofs?

Yes. With the right thickness and overlay, XPS supports foot traffic and roof finishing materials.
