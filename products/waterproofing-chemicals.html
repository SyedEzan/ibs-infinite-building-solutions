Chemicals

Concrete protection and membrane reinforcement

These chemicals are formulated to improve substrate durability, reduce water penetration, and reinforce applied waterproofing systems.

Performance advantages

  • Improves adhesion for coating and membrane systems
  • Reduces concrete permeability and moisture migration
  • Enhances surface durability against weathering
  • Works with repair compounds and reinforcement systems

Product snapshot

A complementary product range for substrate preparation and waterproofing enhancement.

  • System types: primers, admixtures, and surface treatments
  • Application: brush, roll, spray, or admixture
  • Suitable for new and remedial work
  • Improves long-term performance of waterproofing systems

Common uses

Used to prepare concrete, reduce permeability, and reinforce applied waterproofing on decks, basements, and structural elements.

Concrete treatment

Reduces water uptake and strengthens substrate.

Membrane primers

Improves membrane bonding and system longevity.

Surface sealers

Protects treated surfaces from moisture and abrasion.

Why IBS specifies these chemicals

Material compatibility

Chosen to work with concrete, mortar, and membrane systems.

Durability boost

Delivers improved resistance against moisture and freeze-thaw cycles.

System performance

Helps waterproofing systems perform more reliably over time.

Application guidelines

  • Prepare substrates clean and free of loose material
  • Use the right product for primer, admixture, or surface treatment
  • Follow mixing and curing instructions carefully
  • Coordinate with the waterproofing system specification

Frequently asked questions

Can these chemicals be used on damp concrete?

Some products are designed for slightly damp concrete, but proper surface preparation is crucial.

Do these chemicals replace waterproofing membranes?

They are typically used together with membranes to improve adhesion and substrate durability.

Are they suitable for exterior surfaces?

Yes, most are formulated for exterior use where stronger moisture resistance is needed.
