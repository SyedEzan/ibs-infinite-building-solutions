Heat Reflective Coating

A high-performance roof coating that reflects solar heat, improves rooftop comfort, and extends roof life with UV-resistant protection.

Request Quote Browse Other Products
Waterproofing

Cool roof protection with reflective performance

This coating is formulated to reflect heat from the sun, reduce rooftop temperatures, and protect surfaces from UV damage and water ingress.

Product benefits

  • Reflects solar radiation to lower roof temperatures
  • UV-stable finish that extends coating life
  • Improves building energy performance
  • Can be applied over prepared concrete, metal, and membrane roofs

Product highlights

A reflective coating engineered for hot climates and roof renovation projects.

  • Solar reflectance: up to 85%
  • Coverage: 0.9–1.2 L/m² per coat
  • Drying time: 3–4 hours
  • Service life: 5+ years with proper maintenance

Ideal applications

Suitable for exposed roofs, terrace slabs, carports, and parapets where heat reflection and roof protection are essential.

Concrete roofs

Forms a reflective top coat for concrete and screed surfaces.

Metal roofs

Improves thermal comfort on metal sheet roofs and lowers heat transfer.

Terrace decks

Protects terrace surfaces while reducing surface temperature rise.

Why this coating matters

Lower heat load

Reflects sunlight before it becomes heat inside the building.

UV defense

Protects roof surfaces from fading and premature breakdown.

Improved comfort

Helps keep upper floors and roof spaces cooler in summer.

Application guidelines

  • Surface must be clean, dry, and free of loose material
  • Apply two coats for full reflectivity and coverage
  • Allow recommended drying time between coats
  • Inspect roofs regularly and recoat as needed

Frequently asked questions

Will this coating work on existing waterproofing?

Yes, when the existing waterproofing is sound and the surface is properly prepared.

Does it reduce indoor temperatures?

Yes. By reflecting solar heat, it can lower roof surface temperatures and reduce heat transfer.

How often should it be reapplied?

Inspect annually; most applications last 5+ years depending on roof exposure.
