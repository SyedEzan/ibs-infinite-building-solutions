Waterproofing

Which waterproofing system is suitable for my property?
The suitable system depends on the application area, surface condition, water exposure and construction stage. IBS's treatment schedule includes PU membranes for top roofs and external retaining walls, crystalline systems for specified concrete areas, and cementitious coatings for tanks and washrooms. An inspection helps confirm the appropriate scope.
Can IBS waterproof an already leaking roof?
An existing roof can be assessed for treatment. We inspect the current surface, cracks, joints, drainage and previous waterproofing before recommending preparation and a suitable system. Some areas may require repair or removal of damaged material before application.
Do you provide internal and external basement waterproofing?
Yes. IBS specifies a PU polyurethane elastomeric membrane for external basement retaining walls and crystalline waterproofing for internal basement walls and floors. Access, concrete condition and the source of seepage help determine the work required.
Do you waterproof underground and overhead water tanks?
Yes. IBS specifies cementitious coatings for underground and overhead water tanks. Underground-tank treatment can include internal and external surfaces. For drinking-water storage, the selected product must be suitable for potable-water contact and used according to its instructions.
Can washrooms be waterproofed before tiling?
Yes. IBS specifies cementitious coating for washroom waterproofing. The treatment should be planned around floor levels, corners, drains, pipe penetrations and the proposed finish. The inspection and construction stage help determine the required scope.
How long does waterproofing last?
Service life depends on the selected product, preparation, application, exposure and maintenance. Drainage and later damage can also affect performance. IBS can explain the relevant care requirements after recommending a system for your property.
Can waterproofing be applied during rain?
Application depends on the selected product's surface-moisture, weather and curing requirements. Rain can affect preparation or application, so scheduling must suit the system. Contact IBS to assess the leakage and discuss an appropriate treatment window.
What is the cost of waterproofing in Karachi?
Cost depends on the surface area, existing condition, and system chosen: Polyurethane, Bituminous, Crystalline, or Acrylic. IBS offers a free site survey to assess your specific situation and provide an accurate, no-obligation quote.

Termite Proofing

When should I discuss termite treatment for a new building?
Contact IBS during planning or before relevant foundation and flooring areas are covered. We can review the construction stage and explain suitable treatment timing. If construction is further along, available options depend on site access and the building layout.
What preparation is needed for an occupied home?
Preparation depends on the selected product and application method. Before treatment, ask about access, moving belongings, protecting food, precautions for children and pets, and re-entry instructions. Follow the product-specific guidance provided by the application team.
What is the difference between termite treatment and termite proofing?
Termite treatment addresses identified activity, while termite proofing usually refers to preventive measures intended to reduce entry risk. The recommended approach depends on your construction stage, site conditions and inspection findings.
How often should termite treatment be repeated?
There is no single repeat-treatment interval for every property. The product, application method, site changes and inspection findings influence the need for further work. Ask IBS for monitoring guidance for your treatment, and arrange an inspection if new signs appear.
Can termite treatment be done in occupied homes?
Yes. We plan treatments around occupants and explain the preparation and safety steps before work begins.
How long does termite proofing last in Pakistan?
Treatment life depends on the method, site conditions, and later disturbance to the treated area. We assess your property and explain the recommended treatment and follow-up requirements.

Thermal Insulation

How much can XPS roof insulation reduce my electricity bill in Karachi?
Helps reduce roof heat transfer and cooling demand. Results depend on the building, insulation thickness and installation. In Karachi's hot climate, where rooftop heat is the primary driver of high summer electricity bills, this translates into meaningful monthly savings on air conditioning. The exact reduction depends on the size of your roof, the thickness of the insulation installed, and how much of your cooling load comes from rooftop heat gain.
How long does XPS insulation last on a roof in Pakistan?
XPS (extruded polystyrene) is a highly durable material that is resistant to moisture and compression. When properly installed and protected, INSUGREEN XPS insulation can last for decades without losing its insulating performance. Unlike some other insulation materials, XPS does not absorb water, so it maintains its thermal resistance even in humid conditions like those found in Karachi.
Can XPS insulation be installed on an existing roof or only during construction?
XPS insulation can be installed on both new and existing roofs. For new buildings, the boards are typically installed as part of the roof construction. For existing buildings, the insulation can be applied on top of the roof slab and then covered with a protective screed or waterproofing layer. IBS assesses your roof condition and recommends the most suitable installation approach for your building.
Is XPS insulation suitable for cold storage and poultry farms in Pakistan?
Yes. INSUGREEN XPS insulation is widely used in cold storages, poultry farms, and dairy farms across Pakistan. These facilities need to maintain consistent internal temperatures, and XPS boards provide excellent thermal resistance while being moisture-resistant and easy to clean. This helps reduce energy costs and keeps produce, poultry, and dairy products at the right temperature.

Pest Control

How long does a pest control treatment take?
Timing depends on the property size, pest type, access and selected method. IBS can provide a time estimate after reviewing the treatment scope. Some problems require further visits and monitoring.
What preparation is needed before treatment?
Preparation depends on the treatment. Ask about storing food, clearing access, moving belongings and precautions for children and pets. Bed bug preparation can differ from kitchen pest treatment, so follow the checklist provided for your visit.
When can I return to my property after treatment?
Re-entry depends on the product, application method and site conditions. Follow the specific instructions given by the treatment team. Do not use a general waiting time as a substitute for the product's re-entry requirements.
Do you offer scheduled pest control for businesses?
Yes. IBS offers scheduled pest management enquiries for restaurants, offices, warehouses and shops. The visit frequency and treatment scope are discussed according to the pest activity and business requirements.
