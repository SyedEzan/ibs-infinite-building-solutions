Termite Proofing

Chemical anti-termite treatment creating protective barriers in soil, masonry, wood & fixtures — preventing underground termite infestation.

🏗️ Pre-Construction Treatment

Foundation stage: treat all bottom surfaces, column pit sides & basement floors. Flooring stage: treat sub-grade surface @ 4L/m².

🏠 Post-Construction Treatment

Drill 18-23mm holes, 2-3 ft apart in inside & outside walls. Inject Mirage solution forming inner & outer barriers down to soil beneath floors.

10+ Year Guarantee | Chemical Barriers | Bait Systems
