Know the Cause

Why Buildings Get Damp in Karachi

Damp rarely appears for no reason. These are the most common causes we find in homes and buildings across Karachi — and each one has a lasting fix.

Monsoon Rain

Heavy seasonal rain soaks roofs, terraces and walls, and any weak spot lets water in.

Roof Seepage

Water finds its way through small cracks, worn coatings and poorly sealed joints on the roof.

Rising Damp

Moisture from the ground climbs up through walls and floors, leaving tide marks and peeling paint near the base.

Bathroom & Tank Leaks

Hidden leaks behind tiles, under sinks and around water tanks can dampen walls for months before they show.

Aging Buildings & Worn Coatings

Older buildings and coatings that were never renewed lose their protection, so water gets in more easily.

Poor Drainage

Blocked roof drains and gutters let rainwater pool on the roof instead of draining away.

The Karachi Problem

Monsoon rain plus older construction makes damp a recurring problem in Karachi. Roofs and walls that cope in the dry months are tested every monsoon — and a small leak left alone becomes a big repair.

Season by Season

Protect Your Building, Season by Season

A little preparation before the monsoon saves a lot of repair after it. Here is a simple seasonal checklist for Karachi buildings.

May – June

Pre-Monsoon

  • Check the roof and terrace for cracks and worn coating
  • Clear drains and gutters so rainwater can run off
  • Inspect waterproofing before the rains arrive
July – September

Monsoon

  • Watch for new leaks and damp patches after heavy rain
  • Keep drains clear through the season
  • Note where water enters, so it can be fixed at the source
October – November

Post-Monsoon

  • Inspect the roof and walls for damage after the rains
  • Repair small issues before they spread
  • Plan insulation before winter, when roof work is easier

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about waterproofing, termite proofing, insulation, and pest control in Pakistan.

What is the cost of waterproofing in Karachi?

Cost depends on the surface area, existing condition, and system chosen: Polyurethane, Bituminous, Crystalline, or Acrylic. IBS offers a free consultation to assess your specific situation and provide an accurate, no-obligation quote.

How long does termite proofing last in Pakistan?

Treatment life depends on the method, site conditions, and later disturbance to the treated area. We assess your property and explain the recommended treatment and follow-up requirements.

Can XPS insulation really reduce electricity bills?

Yes, significantly. INSUGREEN XPS boards reduce heat entering through your roof and walls, lowering roof heat gain. In Pakistan's climate, where rooftop heat is the primary driver of high summer bills, this translates into real monthly savings.
