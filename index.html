FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about waterproofing, termite proofing, insulation, and pest control in Pakistan.

What is the cost of waterproofing in Karachi?

Cost depends on the surface area, existing condition, and system chosen: Polyurethane, Bituminous, Crystalline, or Acrylic. IBS offers a free site survey to assess your specific situation and provide an accurate, no-obligation quote.

How long does termite proofing last in Pakistan?

Treatment life depends on the method, site conditions, and later disturbance to the treated area. We assess your property and explain the recommended treatment and follow-up requirements.

Can XPS insulation really reduce electricity bills?

Yes, significantly. INSUGREEN XPS boards reduce heat entering through your roof and walls by up to lower roof heat gain. In Pakistan's climate, where rooftop heat is the primary driver of high summer bills, this translates into real monthly savings.

Does IBS serve cities outside Karachi?

Yes. IBS provides waterproofing, termite proofing, insulation, and pest control across all of Pakistan, including Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, and other cities.

What types of buildings does IBS work on?

We work on all building types: homes, apartments, offices, commercial plazas, warehouses, factories, hospitals, and schools. Our solutions are customized to the specific demands of each structure.
