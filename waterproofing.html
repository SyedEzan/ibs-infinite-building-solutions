Know the Warning Signs

Signs Your Property Needs an Inspection

Water stains, damaged paint and recurring leaks can indicate moisture problems. An inspection helps identify the source and assess the required treatment.

Damp Patches & Water Stains

Brown ceiling marks or damp wall patches, especially after rain, are signs worth inspecting. Their location and timing can help identify where moisture is entering.

Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does roof waterproofing last in Karachi?
The lifespan depends on the specific product, surface preparation, exposure, drainage and maintenance. After inspecting your roof, IBS can explain the recommended system and its maintenance requirements.
Can waterproofing be applied on an already leaking roof?
Often, yes, but the leak source and roof condition must first be assessed. Cracks, weak joints or damaged surfaces may need repair and preparation before a suitable waterproofing system is applied.
What is the difference between Polyurethane and Bituminous waterproofing?
Liquid-applied Polyurethane systems can form a continuous membrane, while Bituminous systems include bitumen-based coatings and membranes. The suitable choice depends on the specific product, surface, exposure and application requirements.
Can waterproofing be applied during the monsoon?
Application depends on the product's moisture and weather requirements. Many systems need a prepared surface and a rain-free curing period. Contact IBS to assess an active leak and discuss suitable timing for treatment.
