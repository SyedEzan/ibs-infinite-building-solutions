Step by Step

What Happens at Each Stage

Whether it is waterproofing, termite proofing, insulation or pest control, every IBS project follows the same four stages.

01
Step One

Free Site Survey

Everything starts with a visit. Our expert comes to your property at a time that suits you, inspects the problem first-hand and finds the actual source — not just the visible symptom.

  • Full inspection of the affected area — roof, walls, bathroom, tank or foundation
  • Measurements and photos taken, so your quotation is based on facts
  • The cause explained to you in plain words, with the options available
Typically 30–60 minutes Cost: Free No obligation
02
Step Two

Detailed Quotation

After the survey you receive a written, itemised quotation — so you know exactly what will be done, with which materials, and what it will cost before any work begins.

  • Scope of work broken down item by item
  • Material and system specifications named — Polyurethane, Bituminous, Crystalline or Acrylic
  • Expected timeline for the work, agreed with you in advance
In writing No hidden charges Take your time to decide
03
Step Three

Professional Application

Once you approve the quotation, our trained team carries out the work. Proper surface preparation and correct application are what separate a two-year result from a ten-year one — so we never rush either.

  • Surface prepared first: cleaned, repaired and primed where required
  • The specified system applied in the correct coats and thickness
  • Work area kept tidy, with minimal disruption to your home or business
Trained IBS teams Certified materials only
04
Step Four

Project Completion & Handover

We do not just finish and leave. The completed work is reviewed together with you, so you can see exactly what was done and how to look after it.

  • Joint walkthrough of the finished work, area by area
  • Care and maintenance explained — what to do, and what to avoid
  • Any follow-up requirements for your treatment set out clearly
Final walkthrough with you Care guidance included

Process FAQs

Questions About How We Work

Is the site survey really free?
Yes. The survey, the diagnosis and the quotation are all free, with no obligation to proceed. You only pay if you approve the quotation and the work goes ahead.
How long will my project take?
It depends on the area being treated, the condition of the surface and the system chosen. Your quotation includes an expected timeline, and our teams work to complete on schedule — no unnecessary delays.
Do I need to leave my home during the work?
For waterproofing, termite proofing and insulation, families normally stay in the home while we work. For pest control treatments, the team will advise you on any short re-entry waiting time before the job starts.
What should I do before the team arrives?
Very little — mainly clear access to the area being treated, and access to water and electricity where needed. When your job is scheduled, we confirm the exact details with you in advance.
What happens after the work is complete?
We walk through the finished work with you, explain how to care for the treated area, and set out any follow-up your treatment needs. If you have questions later, you can always call us on 0332-3070499.
