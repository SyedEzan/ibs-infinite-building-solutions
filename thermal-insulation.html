Why It Matters

Roof Insulation in Karachi for Lower Energy Bills

In Karachi, Pakistan, the summer sun is relentless. From May through September, rooftop temperatures can soar, and unprotected roof slabs absorb that heat throughout the day, then release it into the building at night. This means your air conditioner has to work harder and run longer just to keep the indoor temperature comfortable, which directly drives up your electricity bill. In a city where electricity costs are already a major household expense, this constant heat gain is a problem that many property owners overlook.

At IBS Infinite Building Solutions, we solve this problem with INSUGREEN XPS extruded polystyrene insulation boards. XPS is a premium rigid insulation material that blocks heat transfer through roofs and walls, keeping the interior cooler in summer and warmer in winter. INSUGREEN, launched in 2017, is a certified XPS board that is moisture-resistant, durable, and well suited to Karachi's humid coastal conditions. By installing the right thickness of XPS insulation, we can reduce heat entering your building by up to 40%, which translates into real, measurable savings on your cooling costs year after year.

Warning Signs

Signs Your Building Needs Thermal Insulation in Karachi

High Summer Electricity Bills

If your air conditioning costs spike dramatically every summer, your roof is likely absorbing and releasing heat into your building. XPS insulation directly addresses this by blocking heat transfer at the source.

Roof Feels Hot to the Touch

If the ceiling of your top floor feels warm or hot to the touch during the day, heat is passing directly through the roof slab. This is a clear sign that your roof has no effective thermal barrier.

Top Floor Is Always Warmer

If the rooms on the top floor are consistently several degrees warmer than the rest of the house, even with the AC running, the roof is the main source of that extra heat. Insulation can help equalize the temperature.

AC Runs Constantly Without Cooling

If your air conditioner runs almost non-stop but the room never feels properly cool, the heat gain from the roof may be overwhelming the cooling capacity. Reducing that heat load with insulation can make a dramatic difference.

Cold Storage or Farm Temperature Fluctuations

For cold storages, poultry farms, and dairy farms, if maintaining the required temperature is difficult or energy-intensive, XPS insulation can help stabilize the internal environment and reduce running costs.

Uncomfortable Indoor Temperatures Year-Round

If your building is too hot in summer and too cold in winter, it lacks proper thermal insulation. XPS boards help maintain a more consistent indoor temperature throughout the year.

Pricing Guidance

How Much Does Roof Insulation Cost in Karachi?

The cost of roof insulation in Karachi depends on several factors, and there is no single fixed price. The most important factor is the total roof area to be insulated — a larger roof requires more XPS boards and more installation time. The thickness of the insulation also matters: a thicker board provides better thermal resistance but costs more per square foot. The condition of the existing roof and whether any surface preparation is needed before installation will also affect the final cost.

For existing buildings, the installation method plays a role too. Insulation applied on top of the roof slab with a protective screed or waterproofing layer involves more work than installing boards during new construction. Rather than quoting a fixed rate, IBS recommends a free site survey so our team can measure your roof, assess the condition, and recommend the right INSUGREEN XPS thickness and installation approach with an accurate, no-obligation quote.

  • Total roof area to be insulated
  • Thickness of XPS boards required for your climate and building type
  • Condition of the existing roof and any preparation work needed
  • Installation method (new construction vs retrofit on existing roof)

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can XPS roof insulation reduce my electricity bill in Karachi?

INSUGREEN XPS boards can reduce heat transfer through your roof by up to 40%. In Karachi's hot climate, where rooftop heat is the primary driver of high summer electricity bills, this translates into meaningful monthly savings on air conditioning. The exact reduction depends on the size of your roof, the thickness of the insulation installed, and how much of your cooling load comes from rooftop heat gain.

How long does XPS insulation last on a roof in Pakistan?

XPS (extruded polystyrene) is a highly durable material that is resistant to moisture and compression. When properly installed and protected, INSUGREEN XPS insulation can last for decades without losing its insulating performance. Unlike some other insulation materials, XPS does not absorb water, so it maintains its thermal resistance even in humid conditions like those found in Karachi.

Can XPS insulation be installed on an existing roof or only during construction?

XPS insulation can be installed on both new and existing roofs. For new buildings, the boards are typically installed as part of the roof construction. For existing buildings, the insulation can be applied on top of the roof slab and then covered with a protective screed or waterproofing layer. IBS assesses your roof condition and recommends the most suitable installation approach for your building.

Is XPS insulation suitable for cold storage and poultry farms in Pakistan?

Yes. INSUGREEN XPS insulation is widely used in cold storages, poultry farms, and dairy farms across Pakistan. These facilities need to maintain consistent internal temperatures, and XPS boards provide excellent thermal resistance while being moisture-resistant and easy to clean. This helps reduce energy costs and keeps produce, poultry, and dairy products at the right temperature.
