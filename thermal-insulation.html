Why It Matters

Roof Insulation in Karachi for Lower Energy Bills

In Karachi's climate, the roof is the primary source of heat gain in a building. Unprotected roof slabs absorb heat during the day and release it indoors, forcing air conditioners to work harder. Roof insulation using extruded polystyrene (XPS) boards helps reduce the transfer of heat and keeps indoor temperatures more comfortable.

At IBS, we install INSUGREEN XPS boards — a premium extruded polystyrene insulation launched in 2017. It is moisture-resistant and long-lasting, making it well suited to roof and wall insulation in Karachi's conditions.

Roof Insulation

XPS boards installed under roof slabs to reduce heat transfer from the top of the building.

Wall Thermal Insulation

Thermal insulation for exposed walls to reduce heat gain and improve comfort.

Energy Saving

Reduced heat transfer lowers the load on cooling systems, helping cut electricity and maintenance costs.

Can roof insulation reduce electricity bills in Pakistan?

Yes. INSUGREEN XPS boards reduce heat transfer by up to 40%, directly lowering cooling costs in Pakistan's hot climate.

What is XPS insulation?

XPS, or extruded polystyrene, is a premium rigid insulation board that blocks heat transfer. INSUGREEN XPS is a certified extruded polystyrene board used for roof and wall insulation.

Is XPS insulation suitable for cold storage and farms?

Yes. INSUGREEN XPS insulation is used in cold storages, poultry farms, and dairy farms to maintain ideal temperatures and reduce energy costs.

Which buildings benefit from thermal insulation?
